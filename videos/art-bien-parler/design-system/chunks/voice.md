# Voice register: L'art de bien parler (emotional / inspiring ebook ad)

## Creative direction (overrides site DNA)
- Language: **French** (the product is « L'ART DE BIEN PARLER — MAÎTRISER L'ÉLOQUENCE »).
- Tone: **emotional, inspiring, classy, confident** — a premium ebook promise,
  not a SaaS feature list.
- Heading style: elegant Title/Sentence case in **Playfair Display**; reserve
  ALL-CAPS for the product title and short gold keywords only.
- Mood words to evoke: éloquence, confiance, présence, impact, charisme, voix.

## Transform recipe

Take the product promise / value prop and transform with:

1. **Speak to transformation, not features.** Sell who the reader becomes:
   « Prenez la parole et captivez » beats « 200 pages de techniques ».
2. **Present tense, declarative, aspirational.** « Votre voix devient une force. »
3. **Short emotional beats — one idea per panel.** Type sits on glass / over photos;
   long sentences won't read. 3-7 words per line is the target.
4. **Let one GOLD keyword carry each beat** (éloquence, confiance, impact). The
   gold word is the emphasis; white carries the rest.
5. **A little drama is welcome** — this is inspirational, not a spec sheet. One
   restrained exclamation or rhetorical question per scene is fine; don't shout
   every line.

**Examples:**

- IN: `Un ebook qui enseigne les techniques de prise de parole en public`
- OUT: `Maîtrisez l'art de **bien parler**.` / `Votre voix. Votre **pouvoir**.`
- IN: `Apprenez à convaincre votre audience`
- OUT: `Parlez. Captivez. **Convainquez.**`

> Phase 4b scene workers: apply to DOM text only (headline / chip / button copy);
> set the gold keyword via `color: var(--gold)` or a `<span class="gold">`.
> Phase 2 narrator scripts are TTS-bound — keep full French sentences with
> articles and accents; do NOT uppercase or strip words for TTS.
